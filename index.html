The Realm Awaits

When the old ley-lines shifted, the islands of Ark were braided with new magic. The Wizards of Ark cluster is a living tapestry of those changes: every official map bears traces of arcane storms, hidden shrines, and rare spawns that reward explorers and tinkerers alike. We run boosted but balanced rates to remove needless grind while preserving the danger and wonder of survival. Expect mythic creatures, custom mods that add flavor without breaking balance, and a community that values fair play and creative builds. Whether you are a lone wanderer, a master breeder, or a guild leader, you’ll find a place to grow, trade, and compete in seasonal events. Enter the realm, learn the rules in Server Info, and carve your name into the annals of the Wizards of Ark.

How to Find Our Server

  1. Open ARK: Survival Ascended → Join Game → Unofficial tab
  2. Set Game Mode to PvE and search for The Wizards of Ark
  3. Make sure "Password Protected" is unchecked; refresh list if needed

If you still can't see the servers, restart the game and open a support ticket on Discord.

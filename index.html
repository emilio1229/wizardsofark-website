Quick Start

  1. Search "The Wizards of Ark" under Unofficial in ARK: Survival Ascended and join our PvE cluster.
  2. Read the rules in #discord-rules and press the Enter Realm button to unlock the full Discord.
  3. Link your account to Helena Bot (see instructions below) to enable in-game features and shop access.

Helena Bot Linking (Quick)

In the The Wizards of Ark → 🖥️➖spamming channel type /linkgamertag. In-game: open the radial wheel (Console: Hold O/B | PC: Hold R), select HelenaBot → Link Discord, copy the EOS ID and paste it into the Discord command. Helena will DM you when linking is complete.

Thank you for taking the time to register with Helena Bot — this enables shop features, admin tools, and smoother support.

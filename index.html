Linking Helena Bot

In the 🖥️➖spamming channel type /linkgamertag. This will reference you to link your EOS ID.

To find your EOS ID in game:

  • Join any of our servers on ASA
  • Open the radial wheel (Console: Hold O/B | PC: Hold R)
  • Select HelenaBot → Link Discord
  • A pop-up will appear with your linking code

Copy this EOS ID and submit it using /linkgamertag in the Discord spamming channel.

Helena will DM you once your account is successfully linked.

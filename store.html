Store Access

At the moment all purchases and shop interactions are handled exclusively through Discord. We are not listing packs or items on the website yet — shop orders, deliveries, and support are processed via our Discord support system.

How to Order

  1. Join our Discord: discord.gg/84FDUShzE
  2. Open a ticket in #store-orders and include your in-game name, desired item/package name, and preferred payment method (if applicable).
  3. Wait for an admin to confirm availability and provide payment instructions.
  4. Once payment is confirmed, admins will deliver items directly in-game and close the ticket.

We will add web-based shop listings in the future (Tip4Serv integration planned). For now, please use Discord for all purchases.

Delivery & Refunds

  • Deliveries are made in-game by admins after order confirmation.
  • No refunds for in-game consumables once delivered unless an admin error occurred.
  • Disputes must be raised in the original ticket; staff will review and respond.

Questions

If you have questions about shop items, availability, or upcoming packs, ask in #store-questions on Discord or open a ticket in #store-orders.

Store operations are managed via Discord. Check discord.gg/84FDUShzE for the latest updates.

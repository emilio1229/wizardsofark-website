Realm Shop Access

All shop purchases are handled exclusively through Discord. The website does not list packs or items at this time. To purchase anything from the Wizards of Ark shop, you must open a shop ticket in Discord.

This ensures accurate tracking, proper delivery, and clear communication with staff.

How to Make a Purchase

  1. Join our Discord: 💰 Make Your Purchase on Discord
  2. Go to #shop-tickets and click the Open Shop Ticket button.
  3. In your ticket, include:
    • Your in-game name
    • The item, creature, or pack you want
    • Any additional details staff may need
  4. Staff will confirm availability and handle your order directly inside the ticket.
  5. Once processed, items will be delivered in-game and your ticket will be closed.

Important Notes

  • All shop communication must happen inside your shop ticket.
  • Do not DM admins or owners for shop requests — tickets only.
  • Delivery times may vary depending on staff availability.
  • If you have questions about shop items, ask inside your shop ticket.

This keeps everything logged, organized, and fair for all players.

Need Help?

If you are unsure how to open a shop ticket or need assistance, visit #support in Discord and staff will guide you.

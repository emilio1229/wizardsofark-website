Server Info

This page contains the full server settings, rules, mods, and how to join.

Welcome to the Realm

You have stepped through the portal into a living, roaring, magical realm. A full ARK: Survival Ascended PvE cluster featuring ALL official maps, boosted but balanced rates, and active UK & US admin coverage.

Build, explore, tame legendary creatures, and participate in epic events — the Realm is yours to conquer.

Server Rates & Settings

  • Cluster Type: PvE · Crossplay Enabled
  • Platforms: PC / Xbox / PlayStation
  • Maps: All Official Maps
  • Max Player Level: 190
  • Tribe Max: 6
  • Max Bases: 2 per tribe, per map
  • Max Structures: 10,000 per tribe, per map

Rates

  • XP: 1.5× · Harvesting: 5× · Taming: 10×
  • Egg Hatch: x10 · Maturation: x15 · Mating Interval: x10
  • Player Weight: x15 · Cryopods Enabled

Decay Timers

  • Stone: 18 days · Metal/Greenhouse: 24 days · Tek: 30 days
  • Creature Decay: 14 days · Death Bag: 1 hour

Active Mods

  • Cybers Structures QoL+
  • Dino Depot
  • Awesome Spyglass!
  • Auto Engrams!
  • Astraeos – Mythological Creatures
  • Shiny! Dinos Ascended
  • Resonant Shop Mod
  • Alfa Stack 2.5k

Mod Restrictions

Some CS items are disabled (mutator, propagator, cloning chamber, etc.) to preserve balance and prevent automation abuse.

Rules

Building Rules

  • Max 2 bases per tribe, per map
  • Bases must be 500m apart
  • No building in caves, obelisks, spawn zones, or mesh areas
  • Max 10,000 structures per tribe

Taming & Creature Rules

  • No blocking resources or paths with tames
  • No stacking dinos
  • New players (<7 days) may NOT receive mutated or end‑game dinos
  • All gifted dinos must be spayed/neutered

Trading & Economy

  • Mutated dinos must be spayed/neutered before trading
  • Breeding lines may not be resold without breeder permission
  • Monthly Pack creatures may only be traded among pack owners during active month
  • All trades must be documented in the PvE Trading Channel

Gameplay & Performance

  • No hacking, duping, exploiting, or griefing
  • No structures or tames at spawns/obelisks
  • Lag‑causing bases may be wiped

How to Find Our Server

  1. Open ARK: Survival Ascended → Join Game → Unofficial
  2. Set Game Mode: PvE
  3. Search: The Wizards of Ark
  4. Uncheck “Password Protected”
  5. Refresh list

If you still cannot see the servers, restart your game or open a ticket on Discord.

Admin & Enforcement

  • Admins may remove rule‑breaking content
  • Violations may result in wipes, suspensions, or bans
  • Appeals must go through #support tickets

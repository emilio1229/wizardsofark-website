Contact & Staff

All support, appeals, and community coordination are handled exclusively through our Discord. There is no email support. Join the Discord and open a ticket in #support for any enforcement, ban appeals, or technical issues.

How to get help

  1. Join our Discord: discord.gg/84FDUShzE
  2. Read the rules in ⚖️➖discord-rules and press Enter Realm to unlock support channels
  3. Open a ticket in #support and include: your in-game name, server/map, and a short description (screenshots/logs if available)

Do NOT DM admins or owners for enforcement. Do not pull staff into voice — use tickets only.

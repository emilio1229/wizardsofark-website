Full Server Rules & Settings

Below is the complete rule list and server configuration for the Wizards of Ark PvE cluster. All sections are collapsible for easier reading.

Building Rules (BR)

BR1 — Base Limits

  • Max 2 bases per tribe, per map
  • 3rd base = removed without warning
  • Bases must be at least 500 meters apart (measured from main base centers)
  • Main bases may not exceed a 500m radius — larger counts as a second base
  • Exceptions require neighbor agreement and admin approval via ticket

BR2 — No Exploit Builds

You may not build or enter:

  • Under-map / mesh / glitch areas
  • Artifact caves
  • Obelisks
  • Ovis Island (Astraeos)
  • Explorer Notes & Bob’s Tales Notes
  • Spawn zones
  • Valguero Red Zone & entrances
  • Aberration surfaces & entrances (including Valguero Abb zone)
  • Dino nests (Wyvern, Deinonychus, Rock Drake, etc.)
  • Wyvern trenches
  • High-resource spawn zones

No meshing or stacking inside any structures including cloning chambers, traps, gates, chairs, terminals, and Gacha Gavagers.

Violations may result in immediate admin removal without warning.

BR3 — Structure Management

  • No structure spam
  • Temporary build pieces must be removed
  • Max 10,000 structures per tribe, per map
  • Max 8 Gacha Gavagers per tribe, per map
  • Max 3 Ocean Platforms per tribe, per map
  • Max 4 cloning chambers/terminals per tribe, per map
  • Max 3 teleporters per tribe, per map (must have at least 2 unlocked)
  • Tek Replicators must be turned off before logout
  • Oil rigs must remain unlocked (small box allowed for personal oil storage)
Taming & Creature Rules (TR)

TR1 — Tame Management

  • Do not block resources, paths, or access routes with tames
  • Do not leave dinos at spawn points, obelisks, caves, or high-traffic areas
  • Any dinos left at obelisks or inside caves will be destroyed without warning
  • Abandoned, neglected, or misplaced dinos may be removed by admins

TR2 — Tame Limits & Behavior

  • No stacking of dinos at any time
  • Players may not steal, trap, claim, or interfere with another player’s tames
  • Wild tames follow the rule: first hit = claim
  • All disputes require clear proof

TR3 — Starter Creature Balance

Brand new players are here to experience the game properly.

  • Do NOT give A-Tier or S-Tier dinos to new players
  • Do NOT give mutated or overpowered dinos of any kind
  • New players = less than 7 days in Discord
  • All gifted dinos must be spayed or neutered

Giving end-game creatures to new players destroys progression and is strictly forbidden.

Gameplay & Performance Rules (GR)

GR1 — Fair Play

No hacking, exploiting, bug abuse, macros, duping, glitching, cave exploits, 50/50 abuse, or plugin abuse.

GR2 — Player Restrictions

  • One character per player
  • No alts or shared accounts

GR3 — Safe Zones

No structures or tames at spawns, obelisks, or teleport pads.

GR4 — Griefing (ZERO TOLERANCE)

Includes: blocking bases or paths, trapping players, stealing tames or drops, luring wilds to bases, harassment, trolling, provocation, or interfering with breeding.

GR5 — Performance

Overbuilt bases, excessive structures, or dino hoarding causing lag may be wiped.

Tribe & Player Rules (PR)

PR1 — Tribe Size

Max 6 members per tribe, per map

PR2 — Names

No “Human” (any language), no “123”, no random strings, no offensive names.

PR3 — Tribe Names

No duplicate tribe names across the cluster.

PR4 — Tribe Integrity

Admins do not replace losses caused by internal tribe actions.

PR5 — Conduct

No harassment, hate speech, toxic behavior, or offensive language.

PR6 — Advertising & Trading

  • No advertising other servers.
  • No real-money trades.
  • Scams = permanent ban.
Admin & Enforcement (AR)
  • Admins may remove any rule-breaking content
  • Violations may result in wipes, suspensions, or bans
  • Appeals and issues must go through #support tickets
  • Do NOT DM admins or owners for rule enforcement
  • Do not pull admins into voice for rule issues — use tickets only

Trading & Economy Protection Rules

To protect the long-term health of the cluster economy and the value of players' breeding projects, the following rules are now in effect.

Mutated Dino Trading

All mutated dinos traded between players must be spayed or neutered before being traded. This helps breeders protect the time and effort invested into their lines and prevents uncontrolled redistribution of breeding stock.

Breeding Line Ownership & Resale

Players may not resell breedable lines obtained from another breeder without the breeder's explicit permission. If permission is granted, those lines may not be resold for less than the original breeder's advertised price. Respect the work, time, and resources invested by breeders in developing their lines.

Monthly Pack Dino Restrictions

Monthly Pack creatures, whether purchased or won through events, giveaways, or competitions, may only be traded between players who own that month's pack during the active month. Monthly Pack creatures may not be distributed to players who did not purchase that month's pack while the pack is active. Once a new Monthly Pack is released, creatures from the previous month's pack may be traded freely.

Trade Documentation Requirement

All trades must be documented through the PvE Trading Channel on Discord. Trades conducted exclusively through DMs, private messages, or undocumented in-game exchanges are not permitted. Trade records help staff resolve disputes and ensure transparency within the economy.

New Player Progression Protection

Giving away dinos to players who have been on the cluster for less than 7 days is not permitted. ARK is a survival game, and early-game progression is intended to be part of the experience. We appreciate the generosity of our community, but we do not want to eliminate the early-game challenge for new survivors. New players already have access to Starter Packs and other beginner-friendly benefits.

Trade Transparency & Verification

All trades involving Monthly Pack creatures, Shop creatures, or breeding lines must clearly identify the original breeder or seller if requested by staff. Failure to provide accurate information regarding the source of traded creatures may be treated as an attempt to circumvent these rules.

Enforcement

Any attempt to circumvent these rules, exploit loopholes, or undermine the cluster economy may result in administrative action. Violations may result in warnings, removal of creatures, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans depending on the severity and frequency of the offense.

These rules are intended to protect the long-term health and sustainability of the cluster economy, preserve a fair and balanced economy, protect the significant time and resources breeders have invested into developing their lines, maintain the value of Monthly Packs and Shop purchases, and ensure that all players progress through the game as intended.

Daily Restart Time Changes

The daily restart time for all maps has now been changed to: 03:00AM (PST) / 10:00AM (GMT). This time was selected because it is when the fewest players are online and more admins are available to respond quickly if issues occur after restart.

Server Performance Update & Important Changes

To help reduce lag and improve overall server performance, we’ve made some necessary changes to the cluster.

  • Tame Limit Reduced — The tame limit (amount of creatures deployed) has been reduced to 80 per tribe, per map.
  • Auto Destroy Enabled — Auto-destroy for structures and creatures has now been enabled. When structure decay timers expire, they will be destroyed automatically. When creature timers expire, they will also be removed automatically. Expired creatures will no longer become claimable.

Dino Depot Changes

Disabled Dino Depot Features to reduce background processing and lag:

  • Transfer / Upload from Dino Depot
  • Passive production
  • Unfertilized egg production
  • Poop generation
  • Fertilizer distribution

These changes are intended to reduce background processing, which should help lower lag and improve overall server reliability. We will re-evaluate and potentially re-enable some features once stability improves.

Resonant In-Game Shop Guide

The Resonant Shop is your go-to system for earning rewards and purchasing powerful items and dinos — all directly in-game.

How to Access the Shop

PC: Press F1 to open the Shop UI. Console: Use your radial wheel and select the Shop (square or x).

How Points Work

Earn 100 points every 30 minutes just by playing. Points are added automatically — no action needed.

What You Can Buy

Spend your points on dinos, potions, and custom packages. Each package shows exactly what you’ll get before you buy.

Transfer & Map-Hopping Tips

When hopping between maps, don’t transfer too quickly. If you load in and leave again within 1–2 minutes, the server may not finish saving your character, inventory, dinos (especially DinoBalls), or mod data. To stay safe: stay on each map 3–5 minutes before transferring, keep important dinos in cryopods/cryofridges, and avoid carrying all your breeders while map-hopping.

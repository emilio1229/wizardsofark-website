Rules

Building

  • Max 2 bases per tribe, per map; bases must be 500m apart.
  • No under-map/mesh/glitch builds; no building in spawn/obelisks/caves.
  • Max 10,000 structures per tribe, per map.

Gameplay & Trading

  • No hacking, duping, exploiting, or griefing.
  • One character per player; no alts.
  • Mutated dinos must be spayed/neutered before trading.
  • All trades must be documented in the PvE Trading Channel.

Admin & Enforcement

Admins may remove rule-breaking content. Appeals go through #support tickets. Respect the Realm | Protect the Magic.

Full Server Rules & Settings

Below is the complete, unedited rule list and server configuration for the Wizards of Ark PvE cluster. All sections are collapsible for easier reading.

Building Rules (BR)

BR1 — Base Limits

  • Max 2 bases per tribe, per map
  • 3rd base = removed without warning
  • Bases must be at least 500 meters apart
  • Main bases may not exceed a 500m radius
  • Exceptions require neighbor agreement + admin approval

BR2 — No Exploit Builds

  • No under-map / mesh / glitch areas
  • No artifact caves
  • No obelisks
  • No Ovis Island (Astraeos)
  • No Explorer Notes & Bob’s Tales Notes
  • No spawn zones
  • No Valguero Red Zone
  • No Aberration surfaces or entrances
  • No dino nests
  • No Wyvern trenches
  • No high-resource spawn zones

BR3 — Structure Management

  • No structure spam
  • Temporary build pieces must be removed
  • Max 10,000 structures per tribe, per map
  • Max 8 Gacha Gavagers
  • Max 3 Ocean Platforms
  • Max 4 cloning chambers
  • Max 3 teleporters (2 must be unlocked)
  • Tek Replicators must be turned off before logout
  • Oil rigs must remain unlocked
Taming & Creature Rules (TR)

TR1 — Tame Management

  • No blocking resources or paths
  • No dinos left at obelisks or caves
  • Abandoned dinos may be removed

TR2 — Tame Limits & Behavior

  • No stacking dinos
  • No stealing or trapping tames
  • First hit = claim

TR3 — Starter Creature Balance

  • No A-tier or S-tier dinos to new players
  • No mutated or overpowered dinos
  • New players = less than 7 days in Discord
  • All gifted dinos must be spayed/neutered
Gameplay & Performance Rules (GR)

GR1 — Fair Play

  • No hacking, exploiting, duping, macros, glitching

GR2 — Player Restrictions

  • One character per player
  • No alts or shared accounts

GR3 — Safe Zones

  • No structures or tames at spawns, obelisks, teleport pads

GR4 — Griefing (ZERO TOLERANCE)

  • No blocking bases or paths
  • No trapping players
  • No stealing tames or drops
  • No luring wilds to bases
  • No harassment or trolling

GR5 — Performance

  • Lag-causing bases may be wiped
Tribe & Player Rules (PR)
  • Max 6 members per tribe
  • No “Human”, “123”, offensive names
  • No duplicate tribe names
  • Admins do not replace losses caused by tribe actions
  • No harassment or hate speech
  • No advertising other servers
  • No real-money trades
  • Scams = permanent ban
Admin & Enforcement (AR)
  • Admins may remove rule-breaking content
  • Violations may result in wipes, suspensions, bans
  • Appeals must go through tickets
  • No DMing admins for rule enforcement
Trading & Economy Protection Rules

Mutated Dino Trading

  • All mutated dinos must be spayed/neutered before trading

Breeding Line Ownership

  • No reselling breedable lines without permission
  • Cannot resell for less than breeder’s price

Monthly Pack Restrictions

  • Monthly Pack creatures may only be traded among owners during active month

Trade Documentation

  • All trades must be documented in PvE Trading Channel

New Player Protection

  • No giving dinos to players under 7 days

Enforcement

  • Violations may result in warnings, removals, suspensions, bans

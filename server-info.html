Full Rules

Building Rules (BR)

Max 2 bases per tribe per map. Bases must be 500m apart. No exploit builds. No structure spam. Max 10,000 structures. Full text included exactly as provided.

BR1 — Base Limits
• Max 2 bases per tribe, per map
• 3rd base = removed without warning
• Bases must be at least 500 meters apart
• Main bases may not exceed a 500m radius
• Exceptions require neighbor agreement + admin approval

BR2 — No Exploit Builds
• No mesh, under-map, glitch areas
• No artifact caves, obelisks, Ovis Island
• No explorer notes, spawn zones, red zones
• No wyvern trenches, nests, high-resource zones
• No meshing or stacking inside structures

BR3 — Structure Management
• No spam
• Temporary pieces must be removed
• Max 10,000 structures
• Max 8 Gacha Gavagers
• Max 3 Ocean Platforms
• Max 4 cloning chambers
• Max 3 teleporters (2 unlocked)
• Tek Replicators off before logout
• Oil rigs unlocked

Taming Rules (TR)

TR1 — Tame Management
• Do not block resources or paths
• No dinos left at obelisks or caves
• Abandoned dinos may be removed

TR2 — Tame Limits
• No stacking dinos
• No stealing or interfering
• First hit = claim

TR3 — Starter Balance
• No A/S-tier dinos to new players
• No mutated or OP dinos
• New players = less than 7 days in Discord
• All gifted dinos must be spayed/neutered

Gameplay Rules (GR)

GR1 — Fair Play
• No hacking, exploiting, duping, macros

GR2 — Player Restrictions
• One character per player

GR3 — Safe Zones
• No structures or tames at spawns/obelisks

GR4 — Griefing (Zero Tolerance)
• Blocking bases, trapping players, stealing tames
• Luring wilds, harassment, trolling

GR5 — Performance
• Overbuilt bases may be wiped

Tribe & Player Rules (PR)

PR1 — Tribe Size: Max 6
PR2 — Names: No “Human”, “123”, offensive names
PR3 — No duplicate tribe names
PR4 — Admins do not replace internal tribe losses
PR5 — No harassment or toxic behavior
PR6 — No advertising other servers or RMT

Trading & Economy Rules

• All mutated dinos traded must be spayed/neutered
• No reselling breeding lines without permission
• Monthly Pack creatures restricted by month
• All trades must be documented in PvE Trading channel
• No giving dinos to players under 7 days
• Must identify original breeder/seller if asked
• Violations may result in removal or bans

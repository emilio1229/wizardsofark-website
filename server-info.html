Note: Some Dino Depot features are disabled to reduce background processing and improve stability.

Trading & Economy Protection

To protect breeders and the cluster economy we enforce trade rules: mutated dinos must be spayed/neutered before trading; breeding lines cannot be resold without breeder permission; monthly pack creatures have restricted trade windows; all trades must be documented in the PvE Trading Channel.

Rules Summary (Highlights)

Building Rules: Max 2 bases per tribe per map; bases must be 500m apart; no exploit builds (under-map, mesh, artifact caves, obelisks, spawn zones, etc.).

Taming & Creature Rules: No stacking, no stealing or trapping tames, abandoned dinos may be removed, starter creature protections for new players.

Gameplay & Conduct: No hacking, no alts, no griefing; griefing is zero tolerance; performance-causing hoarding may be wiped.

Violations may result in wipes, suspensions, or bans. Appeals go through #support tickets — do not DM admins for enforcement.

Important Notes & Tips

  • When map-hopping, stay on each map 3–5 minutes to ensure mod data saves correctly.
  • Keep important dinos in cryopods/cryofridges for safer transfers.
  • Check #announcements for rate changes, events, and maintenance notices.

Contact & Support

For rule clarifications, appeals, or help open a ticket in #support on Discord. For immediate issues after restarts, admins monitor the server and logs — please use tickets rather than DMs.

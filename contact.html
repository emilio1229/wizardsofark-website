Need Help? Start Here

If you need rule clarifications, to report an issue, or to appeal a decision, please open a ticket in #support on our Discord. Tickets ensure staff can track and resolve issues fairly and transparently.

Do not DM admins or owners for enforcement — use tickets so we can log and act on reports properly.

Support Channels

  • Discord Support: Open a ticket in #support — fastest way to get help.
  • In-game Issues: Report via ticket and include map, coordinates, and screenshots if possible.
  • Emergencies after restart: Use the ticket system; admins monitor logs and will respond.

Contact Form

Use this form for non-urgent messages (it sends an email to the admin team). For rule enforcement, use Discord tickets instead.

Ticket Guidelines

  • Provide clear timestamps, map names, and coordinates for in-game issues.
  • Attach screenshots or video clips when possible.
  • For trade disputes include trade logs or screenshots from the PvE Trading Channel.

Admin Availability

We have UK & US admin coverage. For urgent post-restart issues, open a ticket and include server, character name, and a short description — admins will prioritize critical problems.

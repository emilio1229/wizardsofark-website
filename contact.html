Support — Discord Tickets Only

All support, rule clarifications, appeals, and shop issues are handled exclusively through our Discord ticket system. This ensures staff can track, log, and resolve issues fairly and transparently.

Do not DM admins or owners for enforcement — open a ticket so we can log and act on reports properly.

How to Open a Ticket

  1. Join our Discord: 📜 Open a Ticket on Discord
  2. Go to #support and click the Open Ticket button (or follow the pinned instructions in that channel).
  3. Provide clear details: server/map, character name, timestamps, coordinates, and screenshots where applicable.
  4. Staff will respond in your ticket — keep the ticket thread for follow-up and evidence.

What to Include in a Ticket

  • Map name and coordinates
  • Character name and tribe
  • Exact time (in-game or real time) and a short description of the issue
  • Screenshots, video clips, or logs if available

Urgent Issues After Restarts

For urgent problems immediately after a restart, open a ticket and include server, character name, and a short description — admins monitor logs and will prioritize critical issues.

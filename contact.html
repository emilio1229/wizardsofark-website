Contact & Support

All support for Wizards of Ark — including cluster issues, store purchases, and technical help — is handled directly through our Discord. Our staff team is active daily and ready to assist.

Join our Discord to open a ticket, report issues, ask questions, or get live assistance from staff. This is the fastest and most reliable way to reach us.

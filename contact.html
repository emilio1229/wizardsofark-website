Support & Contact

All support, appeals, and admin communication is handled exclusively through Discord. There is no email support.

How to Get Help

  1. Join our Discord: discord.gg/84FDUShzE
  2. Read the rules in ⚖️➖discord-rules and press Enter Realm
  3. Open a ticket in #support
  4. Include: your in‑game name, map/server, and a short description of the issue

Do NOT DM admins or owners for enforcement. Do not pull staff into voice — use tickets only.
